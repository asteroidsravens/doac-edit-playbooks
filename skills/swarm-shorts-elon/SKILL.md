# Show Shorts — Elon formula

Use when packaging show YouTube Shorts from existing podcast episodes (no new filming).

## What the niche wants
A sentence that feels slightly illegal and already lives in their body:
- My power bill / town / job is being rewritten and nobody will say the mechanism out loud
- Someone is getting paid off my work and I am not
- The race is real and the official story is incomplete

Not: tokenomics, episode numbers, systems thinking as the lead.

## Formula that prints
1. Named famous person OR named fear
2. A heresy (the claim)
3. Completes in ~35–45 seconds (hard cap ~60s for jedit; prefer ≤45)
4. **Title IS the heresy** — never “Ep N …”
5. Ends on a **binary question** (“Are you getting paid?” / “Would you leave?”)

## Cut rules
- Hard start on the claim. Hard end on the question.
- Cold open — no mid-conversation, no preamble, no trailer voice.
- Approve a cut plan (source IN/OUT + title + closer question) before burning edit tokens.
- Follow [jedit](../jedit/SKILL.md) for talking-head 9×16 craft when applicable.
- First frame: face mid-sentence or mascot looking at camera if already on tape — do not spend an hour generating new pet video.
- On-screen text: the title, 4–8 words, huge (jedit Anton caps).
- Description: one sentence + link to the **specific episode**, not channel homepage.
- Hold YouTube until the show owner says go; Drive QA first when that’s the workflow.

## Weekly cadence (day job)
- Cap: 5 Shorts. No new episode. No new host+co-host filming.
- Night 1: pick + cut five source moments.
- Night 2: package (thumb/title/desc).
- Post one per day, not five on Sunday.
- If one clears ~10× median in 48h → next weekend’s 8–12 min packaged cut of that same episode. Still no new recording.
- Skip anything needing new whiteboard, guest, or fresh pet mascot script.

## Shorts = audition
Stranger who lasts 40s on one claim becomes a candidate for the long episode. Title that sounds like an episode label does not.
